Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 06 July 2026

Sanatan Puja respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how the application handles information when you use the app.

1. Information We Collect

The current version of Sanatan Puja does not require user registration and does not intentionally collect, store, or share your personal information.

2. Offline Application

Sanatan Puja is primarily an offline devotional application. Most features including Puja Vidhi, Festivals, Vrat, Panchang, Mantras, Aarti and other devotional content work without an internet connection.

3. Permissions

The app only requests permissions that are necessary for its intended functionality. No unnecessary permissions are requested.

4. Data Storage

Bookmarks, favorites, settings, language preferences and other user preferences are stored locally on your device. They are not uploaded to our servers.

5. Third-Party Services

The current version of the app does not use advertising or analytics services. Future versions may include optional services such as Google AdMob or other Google services. If such features are introduced, this Privacy Policy will be updated before release.

6. Children's Privacy

The application does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any future updates will be published on this page.
